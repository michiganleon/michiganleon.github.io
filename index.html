Xuefeng Hu

Member of Technical Staff·AMI Labs·Paris

I am building world models with real-world understanding at AMI Labs (Advanced Machine Intelligence) in Paris.

Before that, I spent two and a half years at ByteDance / TikTok, most recently as a Staff Research Scientist leading video foundation model pretraining. Earlier there I worked on unified understanding-and-generation models, multimodal reinforcement learning, scalable memory and multimodal LLMs for math. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Southern California, advised by Prof. Ram Nevatia, where I studied how to adapt pre-trained vision–language models to new domains with little or no labeled data.

Beyond research

In 2019 I founded LA Stories (洛杉矶文化沙龙), a non-profit public forum in Los Angeles that hosts talks, panel discussions and film screenings for the local Mandarin-speaking community, with more than 2,000 registered audience members.

Away from research, I am happiest around a table: cooking, hosting dinners that bring people together, and mixing cocktails at Midnight Choir, my make-believe home bar. I play guitar and djembe and love a good jam session. I keep returning to film, photography, painting, writing, mathematics, philosophy and Burning Man, and I am a cat person through and through.

Along the way I have been shaped by the words of Constantine P. Cavafy, Friedrich Nietzsche and 苗炜; the music of Leonard Cohen, Pink Floyd, 张悬 / 安溥, 野孩子, 张玮玮 & 郭龙 and 万能青年旅店; and the talk show 锵锵三人行.

Home has been Dalian, Shanghai, Ann Arbor, New York, Los Angeles, Seattle, San Jose, and now Paris.

As you set out for Ithaka, hope the voyage is a long one.

C. P. Cavafy, Ithaka
